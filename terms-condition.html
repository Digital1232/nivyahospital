DO NOT USE ONLINE BOOKING FOR LIFE-THREATENING EMERGENCIES

The online appointment scheduling system is strictly designated for non-critical, elective, and scheduled outpatient consultations. If you or someone under your care is experiencing severe chest pain, acute respiratory distress, severe bleeding, stroke symptoms, loss of consciousness, or any life-threatening condition:

Immediately call our 24/7 Emergency Casualty Desk at +91 95664 43300 or national emergency response (108), or report immediately in person to our Emergency & Critical Care Department at Tirunelveli.

3 Description of Healthcare Services

Nivya Hospital operates a comprehensive multispeciality tertiary care hospital offering specialized clinical, surgical, and diagnostic treatments across departments including:

  • Pulmonology & Respiratory Medicine
  • Emergency & Critical Care Medicine (24x7 ICU & Trauma)
  • General Medicine & Diabetology
  • Cardiology, Orthopaedics & Dentistry
  • General & Laparoscopic Surgery, Neurology, Gastroenterology, Radiology & Oncology

The website allows patients to view specialist profiles, check clinic schedules, book OPD time slots, and pay consultation fees securely online.

4 User Eligibility & Information Accuracy

To use this website and book medical services:

  • You must be at least 18 years of age or be booking on behalf of a minor/family member as an authorized legal guardian.
  • You agree to provide true, accurate, current, and complete personal and medical history information (including full patient name, valid 10-digit mobile number, and accurate health complaints).
  • Providing false, misleading, or deceptive information may result in cancellation of your appointment and refusal of services, without refund.

5 Appointment Booking & Clinic Working Hours

All online appointment bookings are subject to the following clinic operating conditions:

  • Regular OPD Working Hours: Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM (IST). (Emergency services operate 24 hours daily).
  • Slot Scheduling: Appointments can be scheduled up to 14 days in advance. Appointment time slots are approximate indications and prioritize patients based on triage assessment and doctor clinical availability.
  • Emergency Surgery Precedence: In the event that a specialist doctor is called for emergency resuscitation, critical trauma surgery, or ICU intervention, scheduled OPD appointments may experience unavoidable delays or require rescheduling. Our staff will notify you immediately.

6 Consultation Pricing & Razorpay Payment Terms

All pricing on this website is transparent and complies with regulatory standards:

  • Currency & Taxes: All consultation and add-on procedure fees are stated in Indian National Rupees (INR ₹) and are inclusive of applicable goods & services taxes.
  • Doctor Fee Structure: Standard outpatient consultation fees are clearly displayed per doctor during booking (ranging between ₹400 and ₹800 based on specialty and whether you are a new or follow-up patient).
  • Payment Gateway: All online payments are handled securely through our verified payment gateway partner, Razorpay. We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), major Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), Net Banking (all major Indian banks), and verified digital wallets.
  • Booking Token Generation: An appointment is confirmed only upon successful authorization of payment by Razorpay and issuance of a unique Booking Reference ID (NVY-XXXXXXXX).
  • No Unreasonable Fees: Nivya Hospital does not impose any hidden booking convenience surcharges. The amount shown on the summary card is the final amount charged.
Receipts & Invoices

Upon successful payment completion, an instant digital receipt is generated on screen and sent to your registered phone number/email. A physical official stamped hospital consultation bill is provided upon your check-in at the hospital reception.
